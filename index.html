MyEngDic Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

MyEngDic is an English-Uzbek vocabulary learning application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

MyEngDic is designed to store vocabulary entries and learning progress locally on your device. This information may include English words, Uzbek translations, pronunciation details, example sentences, categories, and learning status.

2. Data Collection and Sharing

The app is designed to operate offline. The developer does not operate a server to collect users' vocabulary or learning progress. The app does not include advertising or Google Analytics.

The developer does not sell users' vocabulary data or learning progress. Information may be handled by other services used on your device, as described in the relevant sections of this policy.

3. Exported Files

You may export vocabulary data to CSV or Excel-compatible files. You control where these files are saved and whether you share them. Other applications or services used to open, store, or share exported files may handle information under their own privacy policies.

4. Reminders and Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device. The app is designed to create these reminders locally and does not use an external notification service to send them. The developer does not receive reminder data.

5. Pronunciation (Text-to-Speech)

The app uses your device's text-to-speech engine to pronounce English words. The engine may be provided by your device manufacturer or another company, such as Google. Depending on the engine and its settings, text may be processed locally or sent to the engine provider. The provider's own privacy policy and terms may apply.

6. Device Backup

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include the app's data in your personal device backup, depending on your device settings and backup configuration. Such backups are managed by Android and your account provider. The developer does not have access to these backups.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Exported files may remain in the locations where you saved them and must be managed separately. Device backups, if enabled, may be subject to the retention and deletion settings of your device or account provider.

8. Children's Privacy

MyEngDic does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is designed to store vocabulary and learning progress locally.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
